Start / reset game

Starts a new game with every non-admin player in one circle and resets all scores. Each player is told their first target.

Inactive players

Players who are not in the current game, such as accounts added after it started. Pick the player they will become the target of. They then take over that player's old target, so the loop stays intact. Only those two players are notified.

Danger zone

Wipes every account (including yours), all notifications and uploaded avatars. You will be logged out and first-admin setup will appear again.
