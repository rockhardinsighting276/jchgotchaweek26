    Rules

    Create account

    Creates a player account. Players log in with the name and password you set here, so share the password with them privately. All organisers share the one admin account created at setup.

    Inactive players

    Players who are not in the current game, such as accounts added after it started. Pick the player they will become the target of. They then take over that player's old target, so the loop stays intact. Only those two players are notified.

    Danger zone

    Delete a player

    Permanently removes a player's account, notifications and avatar. If they are in the game, the player hunting them takes over their target and is told. This can't be undone.

    Wipe the database

    Wipes every account (including yours), all notifications and uploaded avatars. You will be logged out and first-admin setup will appear again. The rules are kept.
